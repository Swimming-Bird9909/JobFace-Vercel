About JobFace

Updated October 8, 2026 · JobFace product guide

JobFace is a small web tool for preparing job-search materials without creating an account. This page describes the features available now, not a future roadmap.

Available today

  • Center-crop an existing image into a square 720 × 720 or portrait 1080 × 1350 JPG.
  • Enter your own resume text and download a single-column HTML resume.
  • Read guides about photo choices, layout, summaries and truthful keyword matching.

Not available

JobFace does not generate faces, replace backgrounds, retouch photos, directly export DOCX or PDF, provide ATS scores, maintain accounts, save work to the cloud or offer paid upgrades. You can use your browser’s print feature to turn the downloaded HTML into a PDF.

How we explain the tools

Guides distinguish product behavior from external career guidance. Regional advice is identified where relevant and linked to its source. Examples and sample profiles are illustrative, not customer testimonials or evidence of hiring outcomes. JobFace is not affiliated with the career services organizations referenced in its guides.

We do not claim professional recruiting credentials, guaranteed interviews or universal compatibility with hiring platforms. Follow the employer’s instructions and review every final file yourself.

Privacy and feedback

Photo processing and resume generation run in the browser. Read the privacy explanation for hosting, optional analytics and local storage details. To report a product issue, use the project issue tracker. Do not attach personal resumes or private photos to a public issue.
