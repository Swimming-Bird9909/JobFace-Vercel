Free Resume Photo Cropper: 720 × 720 JPG

Updated October 8, 2026 · JobFace product guide

Direct answer: JobFace makes a square crop of an existing photo and downloads it as a 720 × 720 JPG. It does not generate a new face, retouch skin or change the background.

Crop my square photo →

Who this tool is for

Use it when a profile or application explicitly needs a square portrait. A resume photo is not appropriate for every market: first read the employer’s instructions. Our photo selection guide explains the US-specific guidance and provides the source.

Three steps

  1. Open the cropper and select a JPG, PNG or WebP up to 20 MB.
  2. Choose “Square · 720 × 720 JPG” and create a preview. The crop is centered automatically.
  3. Check your face and shoulders, then download the JPG to your device.

What the output includes

  • One JPG image, 720 pixels wide and 720 pixels high.
  • Your original photo’s content, cropped to a square; there is no watermark added.
  • Transparent parts, if any, are flattened onto white. This is not background removal.

A concrete example

For a 1200 × 1600 portrait, the tool keeps a centered 1200 × 1200 area, then scales it to 720 × 720. About 200 pixels are removed from both the top and bottom. If the person is off-center, the automatic crop may cut off part of their head: recenter the source in your device’s photo editor before retrying.

Limits to check before sending

There is no manual crop positioning, face detection, background replacement or image generation. Files larger than 20 MB or decoded images over 60 megapixels are rejected. A small photo can be enlarged but will not gain real detail. This size is a JobFace output option, not a universal requirement for recruiting websites.

Privacy and saving

The photo is decoded and cropped in your browser, not uploaded to a processing service. You must save the download; there is no account library. See privacy details.
