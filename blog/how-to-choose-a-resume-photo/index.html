How to Choose the Best Resume Photo

A good resume photo is clear, recent and appropriate for the market and role you are applying to.

Use this checklist

  1. Face a soft, even light.
  2. Use a plain background.
  3. Keep your expression natural.
  4. Crop the face and shoulders consistently.
