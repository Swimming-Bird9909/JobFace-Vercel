Should You Include a Resume Photo? A Practical Checklist

Updated October 8, 2026 · JobFace product guide

Direct answer: Check the employer’s application instructions before adding a photo. For US-style resumes, the Harvard career services resume guidance advises against including a picture. That recommendation is not a worldwide rule and does not decide whether a separate profile page needs a photo.

First decide whether a photo belongs there

Separate three situations: a resume document, an employer’s application form with a photo field, and a professional profile. A platform asking for a profile picture does not automatically mean your uploaded resume should contain the same picture. If the instructions are silent and you are unsure about the destination country, ask the employer or a local career service rather than assuming a photo is required.

Choose an image you can use

If a photo is appropriate, start with an image you have permission to edit and share. A clear original with a face near the center is easier to crop than a group photo or a tightly framed selfie. Look for even light, a visible face and a background that does not compete with the subject. These are practical composition suggestions, not a claim about what all recruiters prefer.

Try a crop, then judge the result

In JobFace, the square cropper creates a 720 × 720 JPG. The tool uses the center of the image; it does not locate your face or let you drag the crop. For a 1200 × 1600 image, the central 1200 × 1200 area is retained before resizing. If your hair or chin is cut off, use a more loosely framed source or recenter it in your device’s editor.

The portrait option produces a 1080 × 1350 JPG. Neither size is an official requirement for every hiring platform. Read the upload field’s aspect-ratio and file-size instructions before submitting.

A worked decision example

Imagine a candidate applying to a US office role while updating an online professional profile. The resume instructions ask for a PDF and say nothing about a photo. Following the cited US career guidance, the candidate prepares a text-only resume. Separately, if the profile accepts a square image, the candidate crops their existing portrait for that field. The two files serve different purposes.

Before-upload checklist

  • Confirm the applicable country and employer instructions.
  • Make sure you have permission to use the original photo.
  • Inspect the downloaded image at its actual size, not only the tiny preview.
  • Check that the crop does not remove important parts of the face.
  • Confirm the accepted format and file-size limit.
  • Avoid uploading sensitive documents or someone else’s photo by mistake.

Scope and source

The Harvard source is US university career guidance, not a legal requirement or a rule for every market. The crop examples above describe JobFace’s own tool. JobFace cannot improve focus, replace a background or guarantee an application outcome.
