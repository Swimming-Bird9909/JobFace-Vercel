Career guides for a stronger first impression

Practical advice for creating a professional resume photo, job-search headshot and resume that recruiters can scan quickly.

How to choose a professional resume photo

A strong resume photo is clear, recent and appropriate for the market you are applying to. Use even lighting, a simple background and a natural expression. Crop the image consistently across your resume, LinkedIn profile and job platforms.

Resume headshot vs. job-search portrait: what is the difference?

A resume avatar is usually a compact square image, while a job-search portrait gives more room for professional styling and industry context. Choose the format based on the platform, then keep your visual identity consistent.

How to make a resume easier to scan

Use clear section headings, concise bullet points and measurable outcomes. Keep the first page focused on the skills and experience most relevant to the role. A simple layout usually performs better than a crowded design.
