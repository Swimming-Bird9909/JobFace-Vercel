Career guides for a stronger first impression

Practical advice for creating a professional resume photo, job-search headshot and resume that recruiters can scan quickly.

How to choose a professional resume photo

A strong resume photo is clear, recent and appropriate for the market you are applying to. Use even lighting, a simple background and a natural expression.

Resume headshot vs. job-search portrait

A resume avatar is usually a compact square image, while a job-search portrait gives more room for professional styling and industry context.

How to make a resume easier to scan

Use clear section headings, concise bullet points and measurable outcomes. A simple layout usually performs better than a crowded design.

How to write a resume summary

Learn how to explain your target role, strongest evidence and next contribution in a short opening summary.

How to use ATS resume keywords

Match your resume language to the job description while keeping every keyword truthful, specific and easy to scan.
