Resume Bullet Point Examples With and Without Numbers

Published November 18, 2026 · JobFace

How do you write a strong resume bullet? Use an action, the work you did and a detail that shows scope or usefulness. Add a number when you can explain where it came from. A precise description can carry the bullet when a number is unavailable.

Choose details you can verify

When you have a count: State the unit and context. “Reviewed 12 inventory records before a weekly order” says what was counted. Use 12 only when your records support it.

When you have no count: Name the output, tool or point in the workflow. If you corrected product labels, specify what you checked them against and when the correction mattered.

When it was team work: Describe your own part. If you tested a new checkout form, describe what you tested and how you reported defects. Reserve “led the checkout redesign” for work where you actually led the redesign.

A quick editing checklist

  • Underline the action and check it matches your responsibility.
  • Name a task or deliverable a reader can picture.
  • Check that each number, tool and result comes from something you can explain in an interview.
  • Remove confidential details and use job posting terms where they accurately describe your work.
