How to Write a Resume With No Work Experience

Published November 18, 2026 · JobFace

What should a first resume include? List your education and two or three experiences that demonstrate the skills the job asks for. A class project, volunteer shift, or community activity can count. Explain what you did and give the reader something concrete to assess.

Choose which experience to include

Examples of first-resume evidence
Job requirementExperienceDetail to verify
Organize recordsMaintained an inventory sheet for a student clubWhich fields did you track and who used the sheet?
Communicate with visitorsStaffed a volunteer check-in deskWhich questions did you handle and which did you refer?
Analyze informationCompared results in a class projectWhich steps did you personally complete?

First resume example for a retail assistant

This fictional applicant is still studying and has no paid work history. The vacancy asks for stock organization and customer communication.

  • Education: List your qualification, institution and dates. Add a module if it shows a relevant skill.
  • Volunteer experience: “Checked in visitors at a community book sale and directed payment questions to the event coordinator.”
  • Project: “Updated a shared spreadsheet to track donated books by category and flag missing price labels before the sale.”
  • Skills: List spreadsheet sorting or visitor check-in if you can explain how you used them.

Decide what goes first

Lead with your strongest evidence for the vacancy. If volunteering provides a better match than coursework, put Volunteer Experience above the coursework or omit the unrelated material. Use the single-column resume layout checklist to check order and spacing.
