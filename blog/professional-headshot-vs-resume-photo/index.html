Headshot vs Resume Photo: Purpose, Crop & Limits

Updated October 8, 2026 · JobFace product guide

Direct answer: A headshot describes the portrait itself. A resume photo describes where that portrait is used. You may need a profile headshot even when you should submit a resume without a photo.

Compare the two uses

QuestionProfile headshotPhoto on a resume
Where does it appear?A profile or dedicated image fieldInside the application document
Who sets the rules?The destination platformThe employer and application context
Is a square always required?No; check the upload instructionsNo universal ratio applies
Can JobFace prepare the image?Yes, if either fixed crop fitsYes, but the resume builder itself does not insert photos

One source, two possible crops

Suppose you have a 1600 × 1600 portrait. JobFace’s square option keeps the square composition and resizes it to 720 × 720. The portrait option removes 160 pixels from each side, keeping a centered 1280 × 1600 region, then produces a 1080 × 1350 JPG. A person standing close to the side may no longer fit; choose a better-centered input instead of expecting face detection.

A headshot tool is not necessarily an AI generator

Here, “headshot tool” means resizing and center-cropping an image you already have. The tool does not invent a person, change clothing, produce a studio setting or replace a background. The portrait shown on the home page illustrates composition; it is not a before-and-after claim about the tool. Choose your original carefully because lighting, focus and background remain your responsibility.

Should you reuse the headshot in the resume?

Not automatically. The Harvard career services resume guidance advises against photos on US-style resumes. Treat that as guidance for the named context, not a blanket instruction about all countries or all profile platforms. If an application explicitly requests an image, follow the relevant instructions or ask for clarification.

Practical example

A designer maintains a portfolio profile and applies for a role asking for a text resume. They can keep the portrait in the portfolio while sending a separate resume without it. In JobFace the resume builder exports a text-only layout, so no image is silently inserted into the document.

Final checklist

  1. Identify the destination: profile field, portfolio or resume document.
  2. Read that destination’s current dimensions and file-format instructions.
  3. Try the square or portrait crop and inspect the result.
  4. Check file size and image clarity after downloading.
  5. Keep the original photo; a crop is not a replacement for your source file.
