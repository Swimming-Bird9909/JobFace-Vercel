ATS Resume Keywords: A Practical Guide

Applicant tracking systems can help employers organize applications, but keywords only help when they describe real experience. The goal is a clear, truthful resume that a person can scan quickly.

Find the important terms

  1. Underline repeated tools, skills and responsibilities in the job description.
  2. Group them into technical skills, collaboration skills and outcomes.
  3. Use the exact term once where it is truthful, then show evidence in a bullet point.

Show evidence instead of stuffing keywords

“Project management, project management, project management” is not useful. A stronger bullet says what you coordinated, which tools you used and what changed as a result. Put relevant skills near the experience that proves them.

Keep the format readable

Use standard section headings, a simple text hierarchy and normal document text instead of putting important information inside an image. Review the final file as a recruiter would: can the target role and strongest evidence be found in seconds?
