Resume Keywords for ATS: Match Honestly, Show Evidence

Updated October 8, 2026 · JobFace product guide

Direct answer: Read the job posting, identify the skills and tasks it asks for, and use the relevant terms where they truthfully describe your experience. There is no universal keyword list or guaranteed ATS pass score.

What ATS means here

An applicant tracking system is software an employer uses to manage applications. Different employers use different systems and processes. JobFace does not connect to those systems, simulate their decisions or give your resume a compatibility score. A simple document can still contain weak or inaccurate content.

Build a small evidence map

Start with a real vacancy, not a generic list of popular words. The US CareerOneStop: analyzing job postings recommends reading the full posting and examining its qualifications, skills and experience requirements. Distinguish a requirement from a preference, and record which parts you can actually demonstrate.

Fictional vacancy termCandidate’s real evidencePossible wording
Spreadsheet reportingMaintained a weekly order trackerMaintained a weekly spreadsheet tracking order status
Customer communicationResponded to delivery questionsAnswered delivery questions and documented recurring issues
SQLNo experienceDo not claim SQL proficiency

These examples are illustrative. The important step is connecting a term to a real action rather than scattering it throughout a document.

Where to use the terms

Put relevant tools in a skills section and describe how you used them in experience or project entries. A short summary can mention your most relevant work, but repeating the same term in every sentence makes the document harder to read. Spell out an unfamiliar abbreviation once if that helps a human understand it.

Avoid the hidden-text shortcut

The CareerOneStop resume formatting guide warns against tricks such as hidden white-text keywords. Do not paste the full job description into your resume, inflate your seniority or list tools you cannot discuss. Formatting advice is not a substitute for meeting the application’s requirements.

Before sending: six checks

  1. Re-read the specific vacancy and any file-format instructions.
  2. Highlight the requirements you genuinely meet.
  3. Connect each important skill to a role, task or project.
  4. Remove claims you cannot substantiate in an interview.
  5. Check the final exported document for readable headings and selectable text.
  6. Confirm that contact information, dates and spelling are correct.

Use JobFace within its limits

The basic builder downloads one text-only HTML resume. If the employer accepts PDF, open the HTML in your browser, print to PDF and inspect the result. It does not directly create DOCX, validate an ATS parser or guarantee ranking. For presentation, follow the layout checklist; for the opening paragraph, use the summary guide.
