Free AI Resume Prompts for ChatGPT, Gemini & Claude

Published November 18, 2026 · JobFace

How do you use AI to tailor a resume? Share the relevant parts of a job posting and a short, anonymized list of things you actually did. Ask the assistant to match the two, draft bullets only from supported facts, and flag missing evidence. Review the wording yourself, then put your approved text into the free JobFace resume builder.

1. Match the job description to your experience

Start with three to five requirements and notes about what you did. Remove names, addresses, reference details, and confidential employer or customer information before sharing material with an external assistant. You can paste these prompts into ChatGPT, Gemini, or Claude.

Worked example for a customer support role

Fictional posting: Respond to customer inquiries, escalate billing issues, and report satisfaction scores.

Fictional notes: Answered 15–20 email inquiries per shift. Sent billing exceptions to a supervisor. No satisfaction scores were recorded for this work.

Supported bullet: Responded to 15–20 customer email inquiries per shift and escalated billing exceptions to a supervisor.

Leave out: A claimed rise in customer satisfaction, ownership of billing resolutions, or experience with a named help-desk platform unless you can substantiate it.

  • Read each bullet out loud and check that you could explain it in an interview.
  • Keep job terms that match your real work. See the resume keyword checklist for examples.
  • Check dates, spelling, contact details, and the employer's required file format.
  • Open the final file and check spacing and page breaks before uploading it.

Where does Claude Code fit?

Claude Code is a coding assistant. It can help developers identify project details they can verify in a repository they are allowed to use. For a technical resume, ask for a file path or commit that supports each proposed claim, then check your actual contribution. A change in a repository cannot establish your personal role or business impact by itself. For general resume editing, a chat assistant is a simpler starting point.
