How it works

  1. Choose the photo tool or resume builder.
  2. Select your own image or enter only the details you want in your resume.
  3. Check the photo preview or downloaded resume, then save it to your device.

Use only photos you have permission to edit. Enlarging a low-resolution image does not restore detail. Always follow the destination website’s size and file requirements.

JobFace questions, answered

Do I need an account or payment?

No. Photo cropping and the basic resume download are free, with no account required. There is no paid plan or cloud saving in this version.

What happens to my photo and resume details?

The tools process your image and resume text in your browser. JobFace does not upload those inputs to a photo or resume service. Download your result before closing the page; it is not saved to an account.

Does JobFace generate a new headshot or replace the background?

No. JobFace center-crops and resizes an existing photo. It does not generate a face, change clothing, remove a background or retouch your photo.

What can I download?

Download a 720 × 720 or 1080 × 1350 JPG photo, or a basic single-column resume as an HTML file. Open the resume in a browser and use Print → Save as PDF if your application accepts PDF.

Should I put a photo on my resume?

Requirements vary by employer and country. For US applications, the Harvard career guide linked in our resume photo article advises against a resume photo. Check the specific application instructions before adding one. A profile photo is a separate use case.
