Free Basic Resume Builder: Download HTML

Updated October 8, 2026 · JobFace product guide

Direct answer: JobFace creates one basic, text-only resume layout. Enter your own details and download an HTML file. For a PDF, open that file in a browser and choose Print → Save as PDF.

Build my resume →

What you can include

The form has fields for your name, target role, contact details, summary, experience or projects, education and skills. Only your name is required. Blank optional sections are left out of the exported resume. Separate entries with line breaks and include dates where helpful.

Build and download

  1. Collect accurate dates, roles, qualifications and examples of your work.
  2. Enter the details relevant to the application. Do not include information you do not want to share.
  3. Download the HTML resume and open it locally in your browser.
  4. Review spelling and line breaks. If needed, print to PDF, turn off browser headers and footers, and check every page.
  5. Upload only a file format accepted by the employer. HTML is not accepted by every application form.

An example entry

Fictional example for the experience field:

Customer Support Assistant — Example Company
June 2024 – May 2026
Updated the help checklist after reviewing repeated delivery questions.
Trained two new colleagues on the order lookup process.

Replace every detail with your own work. If you cannot substantiate a number, describe the action and scope without inventing results.

What is actually downloadable?

One single-column HTML layout, without a photo. The four illustrated layouts are inspiration only, not selectable export templates. The tool does not directly export PDF or DOCX, score ATS compatibility, write your content with AI or save versions to an account.

Before sending

  • Follow the role’s instructions for file type and length.
  • Open the final PDF, if you made one, and check text selection and page breaks.
  • Use your device to rename the final application copy clearly.
  • Keep a local backup; refreshing the form may discard your text.
