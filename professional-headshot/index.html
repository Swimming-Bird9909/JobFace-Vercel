Professional Headshot for Job Search

A professional headshot is a larger portrait for LinkedIn, recruiting platforms and a consistent job-search profile. It should look natural, current and aligned with your industry.

Three choices that improve quality

  1. Face a soft light source and keep your eyes at camera level.
  2. Choose a simple background with enough contrast.
  3. Keep the crop and clothing appropriate for your target role.
